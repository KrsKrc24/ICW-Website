Event Timeline

Every event that has brought us closer to our mission, in reverse chronological order.

2026
September 23, 2026
Chick-fil-A Fundraiser — Illinois Chapter
Chick-fil-A Frankfort · Frankfort, IL

The Illinois chapter hosted a full-day fundraiser at Chick-fil-A Frankfort, running 12:30pm–10pm. With 20% of sales donated back to ICW, supporters could show the flyer in-restaurant or use code CANCER on the app — making every meal a contribution to pediatric cancer care.

Chick-fil-A Illinois Chapter 20% of Sales
September 12–14, 2026
ICW Presents at One Retinoblastoma World 2026
One Rb World Conference · San Antonio, TX

ICW Executive Director Aditi Kuthanoor and CEO Shritan Pothini took the stage at One Retinoblastoma World 2026 — a global conference dedicated to children fighting eye cancer — to present ICW's model of student-led pediatric cancer advocacy. Speaking in front of clinicians, researchers, and advocates from around the world, the ICW team shared how youth-driven chapters can bridge the gap between global cancer care and the children who need it most.

One Rb World Global Conference San Antonio, TX
May 2026
Spectrum Stories: Understanding Neurodiversity
ICW Ghana · Online & DPS International, Ghana

ICW Ghana hosted "Spectrum Stories," a free certified two-day workshop (May 16–17) on understanding neurodiversity and autism, exploring the spectrum through conversation, awareness, and acceptance. The program expanded ICW Ghana's advocacy beyond pediatric cancer. A live "Uniquely Wired" presentation at DPS International brought the message directly to the school community.

ICW Ghana Neurodiversity Free Certified Workshop
April 19, 2026
Sensory-Friendly Fire Station Tour — Illinois Chapter
Tinley Park Fire Department · Tinley Park, IL

ICW Illinois volunteers helped run sensory stations and handed out goodies at the Tinley Park Fire Department's first-ever sensory-friendly station tour, held in celebration of Autism Awareness & Acceptance Month. The event gave children and families a personalized, inclusive fire department experience — and earned a public shout-out from the Village of Tinley Park.

Tinley Park Fire Dept. Illinois Chapter Autism Awareness Month
April 18, 2026
Chipotle Fundraiser — NC Chapter
Chipotle · Morrisville, NC

The North Carolina chapter hosted a "Do Good With Chipotle" give-back night at the Morrisville location. With 25% of event sales donated to ICW, the team spread the word through flyers and the online code 9VC97R7 — raising $195 for children's cancer care.

$195 Raised Chipotle NC Chapter
April 2026
Easter Visit — Sharing Gift Bags at Korle Bu
Korle Bu Teaching Hospital · Accra, Ghana

ICW Ghana returned to Korle Bu Teaching Hospital over Easter, spending time with children in the wards and playroom and sharing gift bags with young patients. Illness is scary for everybody, especially children, and ICW Ghana's commitment to showing up proves that small acts of kindness leave lasting impacts.

ICW Ghana Korle Bu Teaching Hospital Hospital Outreach
February 2026
Holi Fundraising Booth
Community Event

ICW brought awareness and fundraising energy to the vibrant Holi celebration, combining a prize spinner, candy distribution, interactive activities, and education about pediatric cancer to engage the community and raise funds.

Community Engagement Awareness Booth
February 17, 2026
Card & Toy Drive — Illinois Chapter
Illinois Chapter

The Illinois chapter came together to shop for toys, games, and books, then gathered to hand-make cards filled with encouraging messages for children in the hospital. From "Shine Bright" to "You Are Berry Important," every card carried a piece of heart. The assembled care packages — packed with books, games, and toys — were delivered to hospitalized children.

Illinois Chapter Care Packages Handmade Cards
February 2026
World Cancer Day at Korle Bu Children's Center
Korle Bu Teaching Hospital · Accra, Ghana

On February 4th — World Cancer Day — ICW Ghana spent time at the Department of Child Health at Korle Bu Teaching Hospital, donating supplies and spending time with staff and young patients. A meaningful visit that connected ICW's global mission to real children on the wards of West Africa's largest hospital.

ICW Ghana Korle Bu Teaching Hospital World Cancer Day
January 2026
Mammogram Van Tour
Swedish Medical Center · Seattle, WA

ICW partnered with Swedish Medical Center to sponsor a mobile mammogram van tour, bringing life-saving cancer screening directly to underserved communities across the Seattle area. A landmark collaboration with an established medical institution.

$1,000 Donated Swedish Medical Center
2025
December 2025
Understanding Pediatric Cancer — Workshop
ICW Ghana · Online

ICW Ghana hosted a two-day awareness workshop on signs, support systems, and survivorship for pediatric cancer, held December 20–21. Participants could earn a certificate, become a volunteer, and spread awareness in their communities. A meaningful step toward building a more informed generation in Ghana.

ICW Ghana Workshop Awareness
December 2025
ICW × IgniteHerFuture — DPSI Christmas Fair
DPS International · Accra/Tema, Ghana

ICW Ghana set up its first-ever physical stall at the 2025 DPS International Christmas Fair, in collaboration with IgniteHerFuture. The team offered delicious baked goods while spreading awareness of ICW's mission to community members. A milestone moment for the Ghana chapter's presence on the ground.

ICW Ghana IgniteHerFuture First Physical Stall

Sweet Frog Fundraiser — Illinois Chapter
sweetFrog Premium Frozen Yogurt · Illinois

The Illinois chapter filled sweetFrog with supporters for a community frozen yogurt fundraiser. Dozens of customers — families, students, and community members — came out to enjoy their favorite flavors while supporting ICW's mission of pediatric cancer care for every child.

sweetFrog Illinois Chapter Community Turnout

Raj Kapoor Show — Niche Entertainment
North Carolina Chapter

The North Carolina chapter partnered with Niche Entertainment's Raj Kapoor tribute show to spread ICW's mission to hundreds of attendees. $550+ Raised Niche Entertainment NC Chapter

Portillo's Fundraiser — Illinois Chapter
Portillo's · Illinois

The Illinois chapter hosted a community give-back night at Portillo's, drawing supporters of all ages to dine and donate. Families, friends, and local community members showed up in force to support ICW's mission of pediatric cancer care access for every child.

Portillo's Illinois Chapter Community Turnout

MOD Pizza Fundraiser
MOD Pizza · Sammamish, WA

Over 100 community supporters came out to MOD Pizza in Sammamish for a give-back night. A portion of all proceeds supported ICW's pediatric wellness and childhood cancer initiatives, proving local businesses and nonprofits can create real impact together.

100+ Supporters Restaurant Partnership
August 2025
Awareness Booth at Ananda Mela
Ananda Mela Festival · Pacific Northwest

ICW set up an educational awareness and fundraising booth at Ananda Mela, one of the largest annual celebrations of Indian culture in the United States. Through speech, communication, and interactive activities, the team reached hundreds of attendees and raised $800.

$800 Raised Hundreds Reached

Handmade Cards for Seattle Children's Hospital
Seattle Children's Hospital · Seattle, WA

ICW volunteers gathered to create heartfelt, handwritten cards delivered directly to children undergoing cancer treatment. Because healing isn't only physical; every card carried a message that someone out there was thinking of them and rooting for their recovery.

Direct Patient Impact Seattle Children's Hospital

Dance Charity Show at ISKCON
ISKCON Vedic Cultural Center

A beautiful evening of cultural dance performances at the ISKCON Vedic Cultural Center, drawing an enthusiastic crowd and raising $600 for childhood cancer detection and treatment programs. Attendance grew significantly compared to ICW's debut event.

$600 Raised Cultural Performance

Handmade Cards in Bangalore, India
Sammprada Hospital · Bangalore, India

ICW's first international outreach event: a community card-making campaign for children at Sammprada Hospital in Bangalore. This milestone marked ICW's expansion beyond the US and established our international commitment to bringing compassion across borders.

First International Event Sammprada Hospital

Inaugural Dance Charity Show
Issaquah Community Center · Issaquah, WA
