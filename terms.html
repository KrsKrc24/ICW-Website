Website Terms

Updated November 11, 2026

These terms describe use of the International Children's Wellness (ICW) website, which shares information about our programs, events, chapters, and ways to get involved.

Using the website

Use this website for lawful purposes. Please do not attempt to disrupt its operation, access information without authorization, impersonate ICW or its members, or submit spam through its forms.

Health information

Articles and event reports provide general information about ICW's work and healthcare topics. They do not provide a diagnosis or personal treatment advice. Discuss individual health concerns, screening options, and treatment decisions with a qualified healthcare professional.

Events and participation

Event reports describe past activities; upcoming activities may change. Contact ICW to confirm arrangements before attending, volunteering, or organizing a chapter. Reading this website or submitting the newsletter form does not enroll you in an event or establish a partnership.

Donations

Donations are processed through PayPal. Review the amount and payment details shown in checkout before completing a donation. Payment provider terms also apply. For questions about a donation or a correction, contact internationalchildrenwellness@gmail.com.

Photos, stories, and partner materials

This website includes ICW content and material credited to partners and other sources. Contact ICW before reusing photos, logos, or articles, and respect the rights and permissions of their owners. Sharing a link to a public ICW page is welcome.

External services and links

Links to partner websites, social media, and payment services lead to sites with their own terms and privacy practices. Please review those policies when you use an external service. ICW's handling of information through this website is described in our Privacy Policy.

Corrections and contact

We welcome reports of inaccurate information, broken links, or accessibility problems. Email internationalchildrenwellness@gmail.com with the page address and a description of the issue.
