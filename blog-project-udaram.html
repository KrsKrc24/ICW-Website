Project UDARAM: Supporting Prevention in Bengaluru

ICW
ICW Team
September 28, 2026
2 min read

On September 28, 2026, Shobha Trust organized a community stomach cancer prevention camp at Yarab Nagar Urban Primary Health Centre (UPHC) in Bengaluru, India, with Sammprada Hospital. International Children's Wellness contributed USD $1,300 to support the initiative under Project UDARAM.

The camp brought screening, medical consultations, medicines, and health education to an underserved community. This partnership extends ICW's global support work through locally delivered preventive healthcare.

50
People screened for H. pylori infection using the Urea Breath Test. Every participant also received a doctor consultation and preventive health counselling, according to Shobha Trust's report.

Screening and Follow-Up Care

Project UDARAM focuses on detecting and managing Helicobacter pylori (H. pylori) infection as part of stomach cancer prevention. At this camp, 36 of the 50 participants tested positive for H. pylori. These are infection screening results, not cancer diagnoses.

The report states that the 36 participants began a 14-day antibiotic course. They received individual counselling on medication adherence, sanitation, personal hygiene, and lifestyle practices, along with follow-up guidance. Follow-up monitoring was planned to support completion of the course and further evaluation.

Care Beyond a Single Test

The medical team also assessed blood pressure, fasting blood sugar, body mass index, tobacco and alcohol use, and respiratory health. Participants received advice on diet, exercise, hygiene, and tobacco cessation, with individual counselling for identified risks.

Doctors, nurses, laboratory technicians, volunteers, and local health centre staff worked together to deliver the camp. Shobha Trust led the initiative; ICW's contribution helped fund access to the services.

Recognizing a Shared Commitment

In its letter of appreciation, Shobha Trust acknowledged ICW's USD $1,300 contribution toward community healthcare and Project UDARAM. The Trust recognized the support for screening, consultations, medicines, comprehensive health evaluations, and health education.
