Privacy Policy

Updated November 11, 2026

This policy describes information handled through the International Children's Wellness (ICW) website. For privacy questions, contact internationalchildrenwellness@gmail.com.

Information you choose to share

When you submit the newsletter form, the email address you enter is sent to Formspree to process your request for ICW updates. If you contact ICW by email about volunteering, a chapter, or a partnership, your message includes the information you choose to share. That information is used to respond to your request and coordinate the activity you ask about.

Donations

Donation checkout is provided by PayPal. Payment details are entered through PayPal's service rather than an ICW form. PayPal may share transaction and donor information with ICW to help administer the donation. Its collection and use of payment information are described in the PayPal Privacy Statement.

Website services

Vercel hosts this website. The site also loads images from external providers, including Wix and Unsplash. Fonts and map resources are hosted with the website. Requests to these services can include technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the requested resource. Providers may process that information to deliver and protect their services.

Read the Vercel Privacy Notice and Formspree Privacy Policy for more about hosting and form processing. External services, including payment providers, may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Your browser settings can restrict these technologies, although some features may then be unavailable.

Your choices

You can browse ICW's information without submitting the newsletter form. To ask about information you have shared with ICW, request a correction or deletion, or stop receiving updates, email internationalchildrenwellness@gmail.com. Describe the request and the email address you used; please do not include medical records or payment card details.

Children and health information

This website provides information about ICW's work. Its newsletter form is not a patient intake or medical consultation service. Please do not submit a child's medical records, diagnosis, or other sensitive health information through it. A parent or guardian who has a concern about information shared by a child can contact ICW at the address above.

Updates

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date. Questions about a specific request or record can be sent directly to ICW.
